# Taste Skill — Anti-AI-Slop Guidelines

## 1. Eliminate Generic AI Design Hallmarks
- 🚫 **No cliché purple-blue neon gradients**: Avoid the generic "AI SaaS" color scheme.
- 🚫 **No meaningless decorative floating blobs**: Remove random spinning background orbs that cause GPU lag.
- 🚫 **No generic fake stats**: Avoid meaningless vanity counters like "99.9% Satisfaction Guarantee". Use real operational metrics (e.g. "Average 42-min nurse arrival in South Delhi & Gurugram").
- 🚫 **No repetitive generic CTAs**: Avoid placing "Book Now" 15 times on a single page. Use contextual, high-intent action copy.

## 2. High-Craft Visual Identity
- **Intentional Palette**: Curated deep organic forest darks (`#080d0c`, `#0e1816`), warm off-whites (`#f8fafc`, `#f1f5f9`), and vibrant surgical emerald/sage accents (`#34d399`, `#10b981`).
- **Typography with Voice**: Pair modern geometric display fonts (Outfit) with readable, accessible body fonts (Plus Jakarta Sans).
- **Whitespace & Visual Rhythm**: Maintain strict spatial hierarchy. Give hero sections and clinical service cards room to breathe.
